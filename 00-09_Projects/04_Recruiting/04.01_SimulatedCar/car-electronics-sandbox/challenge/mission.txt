MISSION: Bring the simulated car's electronics back to life.

Run test_car, inspect the evidence, make one change, and test again.
Your goal is a PASS and the handoff for the custom-IC activity.

You do not need Linux, embedded systems, C++, or car-electronics experience.
We care about how you investigate, explain your ideas, handle setbacks, and
work together. Asking for help is part of the activity.

Start with the command guide and ecu_protocol. The team directory is team.txt.
car/ contains simplified Python configuration for the PDM and the display.
daq/ contains the logger's existing data and the recovery instructions.
work/ is a place for your notes. Save notes with nano; there is no redirection.

Helpful habits:
  - Say what you expect a command to tell you before running it.
  - Compare the display with the underlying CAN measurement.
  - Share the keyboard and explain changes to your teammates.
  - Bring a specific observation or hypothesis when asking a team member.

At the DAQ stage you MUST speak to Jack, or the person filling that role today.
The facilitator confirms that conversation before the final test can pass.
Describe your diagnosis so far and ask what data format the logger should use.

This is a simplified simulation. Its repair steps are not instructions for
working on a real vehicle's power or safety circuits.
